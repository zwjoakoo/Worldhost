How It Works in 3 Simple Steps

Get your multiplayer world running in under two minutes.

1

Install Mod (Both You & Friends)

Download World Host for your Fabric version (1.21.11 or 26.3) and place it in your Minecraft mods folder. **Important:** Your friends must install it too!

2

Open Singleplayer World

Launch Minecraft, load any singleplayer world, pause the game, and click the new "Host World" button provided by the mod.

3

Invite & Play Together

Copy your unique world code or invite friends directly through your in-game list. They click join and enter your world instantly!
